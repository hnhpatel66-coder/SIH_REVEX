Only owner accounts can publish ride offers. An admin approves the offer before it is visible to riders, and you separately approve each paid seat request.

How a ride offer works

  1. You publish the route, date, time, available seats and price per seat.
  2. An admin reviews and approves the offer. It then appears on Find a Ride.
  3. A rider picks seats and pays immediately with Pay & Book Ride.
  4. You receive the paid request and approve or decline each seat.
  5. Declining refunds the rider under the REVEX cancellation policy.
